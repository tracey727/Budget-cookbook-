# Recommended First Paid Release Scope

Keep V1 commercially understandable and avoid delaying launch with every future idea.

## Core paid-value loop
1. Household size.
2. Pantry.
3. Meal budget.
4. Ranked recipes.
5. Recipe scaling + shortages + swaps.
6. Weekly planner.
7. Combined shopping list.
8. Pack-rounded estimated basket cost.
9. Save favourites/plans.

## Good Free-tier candidates
- browse recipe catalogue;
- basic serving scaling;
- a limited number of favourites;
- manual recipe viewing.

## Good Premium candidates
- persistent pantry + price book;
- “cook now / cheapest gap” ranking;
- weekly budget planner;
- combined shopping list;
- saved swaps/favourites/plans;
- batch/leftover optimisation.

Exact price should be decided after production cost, competitor positioning and payment-fee review. Do not hard-code price decisions into recipe data.
