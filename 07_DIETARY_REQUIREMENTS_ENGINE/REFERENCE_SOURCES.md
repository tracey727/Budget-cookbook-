# Authoritative / Specialist Reference Sources for Production Review

These sources are reference inputs for Phase 2. They do not replace professional/legal review where required.

## Australia allergens / labelling
Food Standards Australia New Zealand — Allergen labelling for consumers  
https://www.foodstandards.gov.au/consumer/labelling/allergen-labelling

Current FSANZ consumer guidance separately lists wheat, fish, crustacean, mollusc, egg, milk, lupin, peanut, soy, sesame, individual major tree nuts, and specified gluten-containing cereals; sulphites have a declaration threshold. Product “may contain” statements are precautionary supplier statements and should not be silently converted into certainty by GENEVIEVE.

## Coeliac / oats / gluten-free claim boundary
Coeliac Australia — Oats and the gluten free diet  
https://coeliac.org.au/article/oats-and-the-gluten-free-diet/

Coeliac Australia — Making a Gluten Free Claim  
https://coeliac.org.au/for-business/making-a-gluten-free-claim/

Production implication: model oats separately and do not import overseas “gluten-free oats” wording into an Australian gluten-free claim.

## Low FODMAP
Monash FODMAP — Starting the Low FODMAP Diet  
https://monashfodmap.com/ibs-central/i-have-ibs/starting-the-low-fodmap-diet/

Monash FODMAP — FAQs  
https://www.monashfodmap.com/about-fodmap-and-ibs/frequently-asked-questions/

Production implication: support low-FODMAP as a structured, personalised/professional-plan mode rather than a permanent generic blacklist.

## Texture-modified food / dysphagia
International Dysphagia Diet Standardisation Initiative (IDDSI) — Complete Framework  
https://www.iddsi.org/

Production implication: IDDSI uses a continuum of levels 0–7; suitability depends on actual food/drink characteristics and testing under serving conditions. A clinician/care plan determines what level is appropriate for a person.
